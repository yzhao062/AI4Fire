  A ratio above 1 means the cluster bootstrap is wider, that is, the frame-level and
  day-level intervals in the round-1 submission overstated precision by that factor.
  A contrast ratio at or below 1 is a real result, not a bug: when the two arms disagree
  on only a few items and those disagreements are spread across clusters, the paired
  difference carries almost no cluster-level variance for the resampling to recover.
